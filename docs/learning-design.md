## Why these interactions exist

| ReasonWeave mechanism     | Learning-design rationale                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | What the prototype actually proves                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Predict before evidence   | Controlled pretesting research found that even unsuccessful retrieval attempts can improve later learning under some conditions. ReasonWeave borrows the sequence, not the efficacy claim.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Evidence cannot be revealed until the learner records a meaningful initial model.                                                                                                     |
| Make an evidence decision | Source labels alone can leave judgment with the AI. The learner therefore selects one sourced finding, reviews its complete ordered linked source list, decides whether it supports, challenges, or complicates the prediction, and explains what those sources directly establish, where their exact scope stops, and what the finding changes about the prediction. In five- and ten-minute focused traces, one textarea collects exactly three nonempty 15–300-character lines for what sources show, scope limit, and why it matters; the 15-minute route retains separate fields. | The learner's judgment and source-scope reasoning are validated, retained, and exported separately from AI-generated evidence labels through the unchanged `EvidenceDecision` schema. |
| Apply evidence to design  | The learner must connect that same current sourced finding to one concrete design choice before reflection, rather than letting creation remain a disconnected response.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | `EvidenceApplication` requires a 20–400-character learner `designChoice`, clears when the selected finding changes, and persists through v4 storage, feedback, map, card, and export. |
| Create under constraints  | Reading is not the endpoint. The learner must turn evidence into a design, model, comparison, argument, or proposal and self-check it against visible criteria.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | A learner-authored artifact is required before reflection; ReasonWeave does not grade it.                                                                                             |
| Explain model change      | Self-explanation research motivates asking learners to make reasoning explicit. ReasonWeave preserves “I used to think…,” “Now I think…,” and “I still wonder…” as learner-authored fields.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | The before/after statements remain distinct from AI feedback in the interface and export.                                                                                             |
| End with three questions  | The system is intentionally finite. It returns the learner to inquiry instead of maximizing chat length.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The completed map has exactly nine nodes and three non-recursive next questions; one learner choice unlocks the final card, with a narrow-screen focus transition into that payoff.   |

The research basis is design context, not product validation. Kornell, Hays, and Bjork reported that unsuccessful retrieval attempts enhanced subsequent learning in controlled experiments ([2009 paper record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19586265/)). Chi and colleagues reported deeper understanding when eighth-grade learners were prompted to self-explain an expository text ([1994 study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1207/s15516709cog1803_3)). Research on lateral reading shows why opening and comparing external sources matters, but ReasonWeave's evidence decision is only a lightweight inquiry scaffold—not a substitute for explicit lateral-reading instruction ([Stanford curriculum rationale](https://cor.stanford.edu/curriculum/collections/teaching-lateral-reading/), [college instruction study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33788064/)).

## Observable learning trace

A completed Discovery Card should let another person answer six concrete questions without seeing a hidden chat transcript:

1. What question did the learner choose?
2. What did the learner predict before seeing evidence?
3. Which sourced finding did the learner judge; which exact sources were linked; what did they directly establish; where did their source scope stop; and how did the finding affect the prediction?
4. What concrete design choice did the learner make from that same finding?
5. What did the learner create from the evidence and constraints?
6. How did the learner describe the change in their own thinking, and what remains open?

That trace is useful even when the learner's model is incomplete. It is evidence of participation and reasoning steps, not proof that the reasoning is correct or that learning occurred.

## Ten-minute adoption scenario

ReasonWeave is designed to fit a pre-seminar, inquiry launch, project-planning block, library research warm-up, or parent-supported exploration:

- The learner completes one account-free quest before a discussion or project.
- The learner can copy or download the Discovery Card and decide whether to share it.
- An educator or parent responds to the visible prediction, evidence decision, evidence-to-design link, artifact, and reflection instead of receiving a score, diagnosis, dashboard, or permanent profile.
- The completed Discovery Card and Markdown export include one optional facilitator follow-up: “What would make you revise that evidence decision or design choice?” It is explicitly labeled as a discussion prompt, not a score or diagnosis.

No LMS, roster, grading workflow, or surveillance layer is required for this hackathon slice.

## Consent-safe pilot

Before making any outcome claim, run two learner walkthroughs and one educator or learning-design walkthrough using only the seeded quest or an approved live prompt. Do not collect names, contact details, recordings, school records, disability information, or other personal data.

Record only anonymous observations:

| Signal                        | Evidence to record                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prediction gate comprehension | Whether the participant understood why evidence stayed locked without coaching                                                                                                                              |
| Evidence-label comprehension  | Whether they could distinguish sourced evidence, inference, and an open question                                                                                                                            |
| Evidence judgment             | Whether they could select one finding, use the exact linked source list to separate direct support from inference or an unanswered question, and explain supports/challenges/complicates in their own words |
| Evidence to design            | Whether they could apply that same finding to a concrete design choice without treating it as AI grading                                                                                                    |
| Agency                        | Whether the design choice, artifact, and reflection remained recognizably theirs                                                                                                                            |
| Export usefulness             | Whether the participant and educator could locate the reasoning trail in the Discovery Card                                                                                                                 |
| Friction                      | Completion time, pauses, backtracking, unclear labels, and any assistance required                                                                                                                          |

Make at least one product change from observed friction before recording the final demo. Report observations as counts and quotes only when consent permits; never convert three walkthroughs into an efficacy claim.
